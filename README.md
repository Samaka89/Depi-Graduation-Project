## Project Overview

**Gaya Loop** is a sustainability-focused website designed to encourage recycling and help keep the environment clean. The platform connects households and schools with recycling collectors who collect recyclable materials such as **plastic, paper, and glass** directly from their locations. The collected materials are then delivered to specialized factories where they can be processed and recycled instead of being thrown away as waste.

To encourage people to participate, Gaya Loop introduces a **reward points system**. Users earn points whenever they recycle materials through the platform. These points can then be converted into **vouchers and rewards** that users can redeem and benefit from. In this way, Gaya Loop creates a simple cycle where people help protect the environment while also receiving rewards for their positive contribution.

The website will present this process through an easy-to-use interface where users can request collection services, track their recycling activities, view their earned points, and redeem available rewards.

---

## Project Objectives
- Promote Recycling: Encourage individuals, households, and schools to recycle plastic, paper, and glass instead of throwing them away.
- Keep the Environment Clean: Reduce waste and contribute to creating cleaner and healthier communities.
- Connect Users with Collectors: Provide an easy platform for requesting recyclable-material collection from homes and schools.
- Reward Sustainable Behavior: Give users points for their recycling activities and allow them to exchange these points for vouchers and rewards.

---

##  Project Scope
- Collection Requests: Enable households and schools to request a collector to pick up recyclable materials.
- Material Collection: Collect recyclable materials such as plastic, paper, and glass from registered locations.
- Recycling Process: Deliver collected materials to recycling factories where they can be processed and reused.
- Points & Rewards System: Award users points based on their recycling activities and allow them to redeem their points for vouchers and available rewards.
- Activity Tracking: Allow users to view their recycling history, collected materials, earned points, and redeemed rewards.
- Awareness & Sustainability: Provide information that encourages users to understand the importance of recycling and protecting the environment. 

>  **Final Deliverable:** A fully functional Gaya Loop website that connects households and schools with recycling collectors, manages recyclable-material collection requests, tracks users' recycling activities, and provides a points-based rewards system where users can exchange their earned points for vouchers.
